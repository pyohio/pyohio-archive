The Python Community Conference Based in Ohio

The 18th annual PyOhio is Sat & Sun July 26 & 27
at the Cleveland State University Student Center
in Cleveland, OH

PyOhio is FREE to attend thanks to the generous support of our sponsors and individual donors.
